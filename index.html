• DESIGN APPROACH

Why I didn't build another chatbot

I explored three fundamentally different mental models — Buxton's Rule applied at the product level.

Option A · Rejected

AI as Conversational Planner

Chat your way to a plan

Sequential interface for a spatial problem
Option B · Rejected

AI as Silent Co-pilot

Ambient intelligence improves search

Incremental, not transformational
Option C · Chosen ✓

AI as Canvas Builder

Show the full trip, let user sculpt it

Spatial visual builder layout
"Chat is sequential. Travel is spatial. The canvas doesn't replace conversation — it gives conversation something to act on."
• CONVERSATION & INPUT

The AI asks — it doesn't interrogate

Single open question to start. Then progressive disclosure — AI only asks what it genuinely doesn't know, in priority order.

1
Destination (or suggests based on vibe)
2
Who's travelling
3
Duration
4
What feeling they're chasing
5
Budget
🌟 Extra: "Past trip failures" — the question nobody asks. Most powerful signal.

Why no other app asks this:

"Anything you want to avoid? Knowing what didn't work last time helps me plan better."

This single question unlocks more personalisation than all 6 standard filters combined.

• AI PLANNING & TRANSPARENCY

Trust is built in four specific moments

01

Summary Confirmation

Before building anything, AI shows everything it understood. User confirms once — no surprises, no commitment before clarity.

02

Transparent Thinking State

A 4-second animated build sequence. Not a spinner — a live log of decisions. Users see exactly what AI is searching and prioritising.

03

"Why this?" on Every Card

Every card has reasoning behind a tap. Hidden by default — the option to ask builds more trust than forcing the explanation.

04

Before/After Swap Panel

When anything changes: greyscale removed card + full-colour new card + 3 specific reasons. Undo available 4 seconds.

• EDGE CASES

What happens when things go wrong

💰 Budget Constraint Built ✓

"Lower Budget" chip triggers card updates. Budget tracker recalculates live. In production: AI surfaces gaps honestly with specific alternatives.

✈ Plans Change Mid-Booking Designed ✓

Booking sheet has per-item checkboxes. Uncheck already-booked items. Agent only follows up on the rest of the plan.

👫 Conflicting Preferences Next Version

The hardest edge case. Production solution: per-traveller compatibility indicator on each day card with explicit tension surfacing.

🔍 No Options in Budget Next Version

AI surfaces the gap: "Cheapest option is ₹X, your budget is ₹Y. Adjust dates or destination?" Never a dead end.

• KEY DESIGN DECISIONS

What I chose to include, exclude, and why

Canvas over chat as primary interface Spatial vs Sequential
▼
Chat is sequential. Travel is spatial. The Canvas gives conversation something structural to act on, making it 10x faster to adjust specific days than explaining it via back-and-forth messaging.
One input, not an onboarding quiz Speed vs Completeness
▼
Get to the canvas fast. Users know what they want when they see tangible options in context — not when filled out in abstract onboarding questionnaires.
Reasoning hidden by default Trust vs Clarity
▼
Option to ask builds more trust than forcing the explanation down a user's throat. Keeps the UI extremely clean while serving curious travelers instantly on tap.
One scenario done exceptionally Depth vs Breadth
▼
Goa, 4 days, solo, ₹25k. Every detail is real. A prototype constraint that is clearly called out to ensure every edge case for this specific traveler journey was designed comprehensively.
Book intent, not payment Realism vs Demo
▼
For India's high-value travel market (₹30k+), a human touchpoint during booking payment is the norm, not a bug. AI coordinates reservation details while dedicated MMT concierges handle execution.
• THE BOOKING MOMENT

One request. Every item. Agent calls you.

What we built:

1

Trip Summary Sheet slides up

All items with checkboxes. Uncheck what you've already booked independently.

2

2 quick preference questions

Special dietary requests + best time to call. Agent gets full spatial canvas context before calling.

3

Confirmation screen

Booking reference card is issued. Dedicated agent messages you via WhatsApp within 2 hours.

Why no instant payment:

"For India's high-value travel market (₹30,000+), a human touchpoint at payment isn't a limitation — it's the expectation. MMT agents close 73% of high-value trips over phone."

— Design rationale
